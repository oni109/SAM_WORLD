    Project blueprint

    Proposal for Samuels Glam & Sam’s Pet World: one website, two divisions, one store. Pages in this demo follow this plan.

    1. Sitemap

    Home (split entry: Pet World | Glam & Makeup) ├─ Pet World │ ├─ Pets for sale ─ Dogs · Cats · Small pets ─ Pet detail (breed, age, vaccines, price, enquire) │ ├─ Accessories ─ Food · Toys · Beds & crates · Grooming · Health │ ├─ Pet care guides (blog) · Adoption & delivery policy │ └─ Visit the shop (map, hours) ├─ Glam & Makeup │ ├─ Services ─ Bridal · Occasion · Editorial · Lessons │ ├─ Book online ─ Calendar · deposit payment │ ├─ Portfolio / gallery · Reviews │ └─ Beauty products ─ Face · Eyes · Lips · Skincare · Tools ├─ Shop (all products) ─ Search · Filters · Product page ├─ Cart ─ Checkout ─ Payment ─ Order confirmation ├─ My account ─ Orders · Bookings · Saved items · Addresses ├─ About · Contact · FAQs · Shipping & returns · Privacy · Terms └─ Admin (private) ─ Products · Pets · Orders · Bookings · Customers · Reports

    2. Feature specification

    Shared platform

    • Division switcher with its own colors and banner
    • One account, one cart, one checkout
    • Search with suggestions and filters
    • Mobile-first, WhatsApp chat button
    • SEO, fast images, Google Analytics
    • Email and WhatsApp order alerts

    Pet World

    • Pet listings by breed, age, gender, price
    • Vaccination and health-check badges
    • Photo and video gallery per pet
    • Reserve with deposit or enquire on WhatsApp
    • Accessories with stock counts
    • Care guides and breed pages

    Glam & Makeup

    • Service menu with durations and prices
    • Booking calendar with deposit
    • Portfolio and before/after gallery
    • Testimonials and Instagram feed
    • Product shop with shade selector
    • Gift cards

    Commerce

    • Cart, discount codes, delivery zones
    • Payments: Paystack or Flutterwave (cards, transfer, USSD), plus Stripe for international
    • Order tracking and invoices
    • Inventory, low-stock alerts
    • Refunds and returns workflow

    Admin dashboard

    • Add and edit pets, products, services
    • Order and booking management
    • Sales reports per division
    • Staff roles and permissions
    • Customer list and email campaigns

    Quality

    • HTTPS, PCI-compliant hosted payments
    • Backups and uptime monitoring
    • Accessibility (WCAG AA)
    • Privacy policy and cookie consent

    3. Recommended technology architecture

    LayerRecommendationWhy
    FrontendNext.js (React) + Tailwind CSSFast pages, strong SEO, shared design system with two themes
    Backend / APINode.js (NestJS) REST APIOne codebase for products, pets, bookings and orders
    DatabasePostgreSQL + Redis cacheReliable orders and stock; fast catalog
    PaymentsPaystack / Flutterwave; Stripe optionalLocal cards, transfer, USSD; webhooks confirm orders
    MediaCloudinary or S3 + CDNOptimised pet and product photos and video
    AuthEmail, phone OTP, Google sign-inLow-friction sign-up
    MessagingWhatsApp Business API, email (Resend)Orders, bookings and reminders
    HostingVercel (web) + managed Postgres (e.g. Neon or AWS RDS)Easy scaling, low maintenance
    Faster optionShopify + booking app, custom themeQuicker launch if the client prefers a hosted store

    4. Development plan

    Weeks 1–2 · Discovery and designBrand kit, sitemap sign-off, wireframes, two-theme design system, content list.
    Weeks 3–5 · Core buildHome, division pages, catalog, pet and product pages, search and filters.
    Weeks 5–7 · CommerceCart, checkout, payments, delivery rules, order emails, accounts.
    Weeks 7–8 · Bookings and adminGlam booking calendar and deposits, admin dashboard, reports.
    Week 9 · TestingPayment tests, mobile and browser checks, speed, security, accessibility.
    Week 10 · LaunchData import, domain and SSL, staff training, go-live, 30-day support.
